Keep your recordings safe

This is a beta. Do not use it as the only copy of an important recording. App data is excluded from device backup. Export important files before changing devices or uninstalling. Do not delete and reinstall the app as a troubleshooting step. The developer has no server copy of your contents.

Before the first recording

  1. Use a supported iPhone with iOS 26.2 or later; the main target is iPhone 15 Pro and newer. Check free storage and microphone permission.
  2. Prepare the required models using the app's model controls. The first download and preparation can take longer than subsequent use. Prefer reliable Wi-Fi and power; check displayed progress or errors.
  3. Choose the meeting language before recording. For German, select Deutsch explicitly. Use Auto only when you want automatic language detection.
  4. Start with a short, non-confidential recording and check that audio and live text are captured. A visible live result is provisional, not a guarantee of final accuracy.

Stopping and naming a recording

Stop is intended to open the title dialog before final transcription. The default title begins with a date in yyyy-MM-dd format. Adjust the title template in Settings. If finalization fails, retain the audio and recovery entry rather than deleting it; note the error before retrying.

The live text stops or changes language

Check the selected language and whether the app reports model preparation, an interruption or finalization. Use a fixed language for a monolingual meeting; Auto is a separate mode. Keep the recording and report the selected language, the last update time, the exact error and the audio route. Do not assume that stalled text means the recording has stopped; inspect the app's recording status.

Corrections and speaker labels

Review uncertain words against their audio and use the transcript's editing controls to make manual corrections. Check the saved result after reopening it. Speaker labels and optional voiceprint matches can be wrong. Correct them rather than treating them as identification evidence. If the keyboard or save action does not work, report the steps and build number without sending transcript screenshots containing private information.

Imports, exports and device changes

Try a small file you are entitled to use before importing important material. Review exported text and timecodes. When sharing, you choose the destination: a cloud drive or recipient can receive the file. Save required audio and transcripts separately before uninstalling or changing devices; a transcript-only export is not an audio backup.

How to report a beta issue

  • App version and build number shown in Settings.
  • iPhone model, iOS version and selected language (fixed or Auto).
  • Microphone route: iPhone, AirPods, other Bluetooth or imported file.
  • Approximate recording duration; whether the screen was locked or the app was interrupted.
  • What you did, what you expected, what happened and the exact error.
  • Whether the issue repeats. Share a diagnostic report only deliberately and after reviewing it.

You can also use TestFlight feedback if invited to the beta. Apple handles that channel under its own privacy rules. Avoid confidential attachments and screenshots.

What the beta does not promise

No universal accuracy, verified three-hour endurance or validated performance in every target language is claimed. Accuracy depends on recording conditions, language, vocabulary and overlapping voices. Always check consequential names, numbers and negations against the original audio.
