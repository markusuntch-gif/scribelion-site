Your recording.

ScribeLion is being built for local transcription of meetings and interviews. Speech recognition and speaker processing run on your device, not on a transcription server.

Help and feedbackHow your data is handled

Record or import

Choose a language, record audio or import a file, and review the transcript. Models may need an initial download before recognition is available.

Review against audio

Work with time-linked words, speaker turns and manual corrections. The beta is intended to test these workflows, not to replace review of important statements.

Keep control

No ScribeLion account or cloud sync. Export only when you choose to. Optional local speaker profiles and vocabulary are kept in the app.

Before relying on the beta

Accuracy, long recordings and interruption handling still require real-device testing. Do not use this beta as the only copy of an important recording. App data is excluded from device backup: export what you need before uninstalling or changing devices.

This website is not an App Store release announcement or an open TestFlight invitation. Availability is managed separately through Apple.
