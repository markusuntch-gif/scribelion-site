Privacy policy · Updated 5 October 2026

Local transcription,
clear boundaries.

This policy describes the current ScribeLion iOS beta and this website. Local transcription does not mean that model downloads, Apple TestFlight, support emails or exports never use the internet.

1. Who is responsible?

The developer and responsible person for ScribeLion is Markus Untch. For privacy or support requests, contact markus.untch@gmail.com.

2. Audio, transcripts and local app data

ScribeLion processes microphone recordings and imported audio on your device. Recordings, transcripts, word timings, speaker assignments, vocabulary, corrections and revision information are stored locally. The app does not automatically upload these contents to the developer, a cloud transcription service or a general-purpose AI service.

There is no ScribeLion account, cloud sync, advertising or app analytics service. The app requests microphone permission for recording. Only record or import material you are entitled to process, and respect the privacy and recording rules that apply to other participants.

3. Optional speaker profiles

If you create a speaker profile, ScribeLion stores a local voice representation (voiceprint) and associated profile information to help match a speaker in later recordings. Voiceprints are sensitive data and are not sent to the developer or a speaker-recognition server. The voice representation uses the device's local Keychain; it is not a cloud identity or authentication service.

Enrollment is optional. Manage and delete profiles in the app's speaker-profile settings. Only enroll voices you are entitled to use. Speaker matches can be wrong and must not be treated as proof of a person's identity.

4. Models and network connections

Speech and speaker models may need to be downloaded before a feature can work. Apple manages its own speech assets; other models are obtained from third-party model repositories, including Hugging Face and their delivery infrastructure. Model preparation may also contact repository or release services such as GitHub. These requests expose technical connection information, including IP address, requested file and request time, to the providers. ScribeLion does not attach your recordings, transcript text, vocabulary or voiceprints to these model downloads.

Once the required assets are available, recognition and speaker processing are local. Assets may need to be downloaded again if they are missing, removed or updated. This policy does not describe those providers as offline or anonymous. Their own policies apply to their network services: Hugging Face, GitHub and Apple.

5. Storage, deletion and backups

The app uses iOS file protection for its private files and local Keychain protection for voice representations. Its database is configured without CloudKit sync. App-managed content and diagnostic storage are excluded from device backup; do not rely on iCloud or a computer backup to restore this app's contents.

You control local recordings and transcripts through the app. Deleting an entry initiates deletion of its local content and associated recording. In this beta, some file-deletion failures may not be displayed; a removed list entry is not proof that every associated file has been erased. Optional saved speaker profiles are managed separately. The app does not promise secure forensic erasure of flash storage or removal of copies you previously exported.

Export important audio and text before uninstalling, replacing or resetting your device. There is no server copy from which the developer can restore your local contents. Exports saved elsewhere remain until you remove them there.

6. Exports and sharing

When you choose an export destination or share through iOS, the selected files may leave the device. Their contents can include audio, transcript text, speaker labels, word timings and other export metadata. A cloud drive, recipient or other chosen service handles those files under its own rules. Sharing a file is a deliberate exception to keeping that file local; ScribeLion does not silently sync it.

7. Diagnostics and TestFlight

ScribeLion keeps bounded local resource and system diagnostics, for example execution time, memory use, battery measurements, thermal state and errors. These are not automatically uploaded by ScribeLion. You may choose to share a diagnostic export; review it first.

If you install through TestFlight, Apple may collect and provide developers with crash, usage and beta-feedback information under Apple's TestFlight privacy information. Feedback attachments and screenshots may contain private content. Do not include confidential audio, transcripts or voiceprints in feedback. Apple's platform diagnostics are separate from the app's local diagnostic files.

8. This website and support email

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not load analytics, external fonts, advertising, embedded media or application scripts. GitHub receives normal web-request information and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments to answer your request. The published address is a Gmail address; email uses external provider services, not local app storage. See Google's privacy policy and the rules of your own email provider. Do not email contents you do not want the developer to read. Support-message retention depends on handling your request, necessary follow-up and any applicable legal obligations; no automatic deletion deadline is promised. Contact the developer to request deletion of correspondence.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose whether to record, import, enroll speakers, export or contact support. The developer cannot remotely access or edit contents kept only on your device.

Where the GDPR applies, responding to a requested support service relies on Article 6(1)(b); website security and necessary support administration rely on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f), and specific mandatory records on Article 6(1)(c). Local features do not require a developer account or submission of your recordings. Contact the developer about access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction or objection concerning personal information the developer holds. Applicable exceptions may limit a right. You may complain to your competent data protection authority.

10. Changes

Changes to data handling will be reflected here with an updated date. New cloud processing or automatic content transmission is not covered by a claim of local transcription. Check this policy and the app's local-data information before using a new beta.
